# Hardware Design Specification: Z Fold8 Ultra → Mocagen MC1 Adapter

Front-facing clip-on tray that holds a fully unfolded Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra
8–10 mm ahead of the MC1 faceplate, clipped to the two handle grips instead
of the 175 mm telescoping rail.

## Devices
- Z Fold8 Ultra unfolded: 158.4 x 143.2 x 4.1 mm (corrected; original brief said 132.6 x 5.5)
- MC1: 175 mm telescoping limit, Bluetooth LE (no USB-C alignment needed)

## Geometry
- Tray stands off 8–10 mm forward of the faceplate (clears D-pad/face buttons)
- Two mirrored wrap-around handle clips, 0.2 mm interference fit
- Vertical support columns along the back plate

| Variant | Bucket width | Depth | Clearance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portrait | 144.6 mm (brief said 134.0; = 143.2 + 1.4) | 6.5 mm | +1.4 mm XY |
| Landscape | 159.8 mm | 6.5 mm | +1.4 mm XY |

## Print settings
- PETG or Tough PLA (not plain PLA)
- Print flat on the backplate; 25–35% gyroid/cubic infill
- >= 4 walls, >= 5 top/bottom layers
